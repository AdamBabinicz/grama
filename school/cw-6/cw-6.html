Ćwiczenia gramatyczne

Matura - Tłumaczenie fragmentów. Przetłumacz podane fragmenty zdań.

Przetłumacz

Ex.1

1. Put these papers into the envelope and (przyklej) a stamp.

Put these papers into the envelope and stick/stamp (przyklej) a stamp.

2. We (utknąć w korku) in Main Street yesterday and were late for school.

We got stuck (utknąć w korku) in Main Street yesterday and were late for school.

3. All my relatives live (na wyżynie).

All my relatives live in an upland (na wyżynie).

4. My neighbours are (prawosławni).

My neighbours are Orthodox people (prawosławni).

5. My parents don’t earn much and we have some difficulties in (wiązać koniec z końcem).

My parents don’t earn much and we have some difficulties in making ends meet (wiązać koniec z końcem).

6. Supper is ready, can you (nakryć do stołu)?

Supper is ready, can you set the table (nakryć do stołu)?

7. (trudno uwierzyć), but nobody survived.

It’s hard to believe (trudno uwierzyć), but nobody survived.

8. Ted can’t afford to buy such an expensive house and (wesprzeć) his big family.

Ted can’t afford to buy such an expensive house and support (wesprzeć) his big family.

9. Have you heard about it from (zródeł pisanych) or was it just (pocztą pantoflową).

Have you heard about it from written sources (zródeł pisanych) or was it just grapevine (pocztą pantoflową).

10. My mum says she is on a diet and has to (spalić) some calories.

My mum says she is on a diet and has to burn (spalić) some calories.
